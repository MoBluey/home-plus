# Code of Conduct

Home+ is a small project, made in the open by people who like their TV to feel like an Apple TV. Everyone is welcome here, whoever they are and whatever they know about webOS, rooting or C.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to webOS homebrew.
- Assume good intent. Disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Keep issues, pull requests and reviews about the work.
- Respect people's privacy. Don't post anyone's personal details, and leave yours out of logs and screenshots too: Wi-Fi names, tokens and sign-ins.

## What isn't welcome

- Harassment, insults, threats, and discrimination of any kind.
- Sexual language or images, and personal attacks.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Asking for, or offering, ways around paid or encrypted apps. Home+ opens the apps you have; it doesn't work around them.

## Where it applies

In this repository: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews. It also applies wherever someone speaks for the project.

## Reporting

If someone breaks this code, or you feel unsafe, tell the maintainer, [@pbalazaar](https://github.com/pbalazaar), privately: by email, at the address on their GitHub profile. For abuse on GitHub itself, you can also use GitHub's **Report content**.

Every report is read, and the reporter's name is kept private. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close issues and pull requests, or block someone from the project, depending on what happened.

This code follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
