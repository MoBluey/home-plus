# Accessibility

Home+ should work for everyone who uses an LG TV. This page says what Home+ does for accessibility, what it doesn't do yet, and how to tell us when something gets in your way.

## What Home+ does

- **The remote is enough.** Everything works with the arrows, OK and Back on any LG remote, or by pointing with the Magic Remote. Nothing needs typing: sign-ins use codes or QR codes that you approve on another device.
- **You can always see the focus.** A focused tile grows, lifts and catches the light, and focused buttons turn white with dark text.
- **Text stays readable.** It sits on dark backings and scrims, also over bright artwork.
- **Reduce Motion** (Settings → Accessibility) turns off the swaying, wobbling, shimmering and scrolling text. Things settle without bouncing, and menus appear in place instead of growing. The Top Shelf can also stay still (Settings → Display & OLED → Top Shelf).
- **Reduce Transparency** (Settings → Accessibility) puts solid backgrounds behind text instead of glass.
- **Focus sounds** (Settings → Sounds) give a soft tick for each move. They're optional.
- **OLED care** dims the screen, then turns it black, when it's idle. You can change the timings or turn them off (Settings → Display & OLED).

## What it doesn't do yet

- **Screen readers:** LG's Audio Guidance doesn't read Home+. Home+ draws its own interface with OpenGL, which webOS's screen reader can't see.
- **Text size and contrast:** Home+ doesn't follow webOS's own text size or high-contrast settings, and its text size can't be changed.
- **Languages:** English only.
- **Playback:** captions and audio description belong to the apps you open, not to Home+.

## Where it works

LG webOS TVs rooted with the Homebrew Channel. It's tested on an LG C3 (webOS 23), with LG's Magic Remote and its arrow-key remotes.

## Tell us about a barrier

Open an issue with the **Bug report** template. Say what you were trying to do, what got in your way, your TV model, and which remote you use. A barrier is a bug, and it's treated as one.
